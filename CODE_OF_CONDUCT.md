# Community conduct

This project welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Treat other contributors with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions and
other project spaces. Give specific, constructive feedback on the work. Respect
privacy and consent.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Disagreement about technical decisions is welcome; intimidation is not.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to [codefriend@agent-logic.ai](mailto:codefriend@agent-logic.ai).
Include enough context for maintainers to assess the concern, and avoid sharing
unnecessary personal information. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.
For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may moderate project contributions and discussions to address conduct
that violates this policy. Responses should consider the facts, severity and
impact of the behavior. Possible actions include requesting a change, removing
content, limiting participation or excluding a participant from project spaces.

Report handling and any disclosures should be limited to what is necessary to
assess and address the concern. This policy does not promise confidentiality,
a particular response time or an appeal process that has not been established.
