## Summary

Remediate P2 finding `S520-SEC-001` from internal review #520 by extending publication redaction and path-portability checks to every document referenced by the publication manifest. At reviewed candidate `c24f8fa65ce445b03ce6cd69007307291d78b60c`, the validator scans only TAIL-03 packet files and hash-checks the 791 referenced documents without scanning their bytes; a complete specialist pass found 35 machine-local-path occurrences while `--all` still reported final acceptance.

## Goal

Make the publication gate fail closed when any manifest-referenced document exposes machine-local paths, credential locations, recognized secret shapes, or unreadable content.

## Required Outcome

The publication validator scans 791 of 791 referenced document byte streams, all 35 confirmed machine-local-path occurrences are removed or safely generalized, unsafe or unreadable referenced content blocks acceptance, and emitted findings never reveal detected secret values.

- Complete manifest-referenced redaction and path-portability traversal in `.csdlc/prepared/issues/519/validate-publication-candidate.rb`.
- Safe normalization or removal of all 35 confirmed machine-local-path occurrences in referenced documents.
- Negative fixtures covering machine-local paths, credential locations, private-key blocks, bearer values, and common provider token shapes inside referenced documents.
- Safe machine-readable findings that identify path, line, and pattern class without printing matched secret values.
- Updated TAIL-03 and #519 lifecycle claims that describe the validator's actual complete denominator.
- A retained 791-of-791 zero-hit redaction result at the implementation head.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every readable document referenced by the publication manifest is scanned from its exact bound bytes.
- Missing, unreadable, hash-mismatched, or redaction-unsafe referenced content fails closed.
- All 35 confirmed machine-local-path occurrences are removed or replaced with repository-relative or appropriately generalized forms.
- Findings output never includes private key material, token values, bearer values, credential contents, or other matched secret text.
- Negative fixtures prove failures for each required pattern class when it appears only in a manifest-referenced document.
- `--redaction` and `--all` independently report a zero-hit, 791-of-791 result.
- Lifecycle and publication claims no longer imply a broader scan than the validator actually performs.
