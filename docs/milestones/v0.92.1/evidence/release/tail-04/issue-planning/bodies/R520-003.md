## Summary

Repair the P2 dynamic-agent health-sweep defect that aborts all remaining checks when one asynchronous health task fails.

## Goal

Keep unrelated dynamic-agent readiness projections current even when one agent health task panics or is cancelled.

## Required Outcome

At exact review candidate `c24f8fa65ce445b03ce6cd69007307291d78b60c`, finding `C520-CODE-003` shows that the `JoinSet` loop exits on the first `JoinError` and drops the remaining tasks. The repaired sweep must explicitly process successes, failures, and exhaustion, preserve failed-agent identity, and drain all remaining checks.

## Deliverables

- Explicit success, failure, and end-of-set handling in the dynamic-agent health sweep.
- Stable association between each task and its agent identity, including panic or cancellation paths.
- Deterministic multi-agent regression coverage.
- Observable failure projection that does not overwrite successful peer updates.

## Acceptance Criteria

- One failed or cancelled health task does not terminate the remainder of the sweep.
- Every remaining check is drained and every successful peer projection is retained.
- The failed check is associated with a stable agent identifier and surfaced as a failure.
- Task failures are not hidden, downgraded, or converted to successful health.
