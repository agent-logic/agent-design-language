## Summary

Repair the P1 durability gap between successful dynamic-agent admission and the required autonomous Beacon greeting by giving admission-triggered A2A initiation durable ownership and recovery.

## Goal

Ensure an admitted agent reliably enters the autonomous A2A flow without an operator prompt, including after provider refusal, task interruption, or Runtime restart.

## Required Outcome

At exact review candidate `c24f8fa65ce445b03ce6cd69007307291d78b60c`, findings `A520-ARCH-002` and `C520-CODE-002` show that admission returns success before the greeting has durable ownership and discards the spawned result. The repaired Runtime must persist an idempotent initiation intent, retry it under a declared policy, resume it on startup or repeated admission, and record a terminal disposition.

## Deliverables

- A durable greeting or outbox intent coupled to successful admission.
- A bounded, idempotent worker with startup and repeated-admission recovery.
- Stable conversation-ledger idempotency binding and terminal receipt.
- Runtime health or evidence projection for pending, retrying, completed, and terminal-failure states.
- Deterministic recovery and duplicate-suppression tests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Successful admission durably records the autonomous greeting obligation before acknowledging a terminally complete workflow.
- Pending work resumes after process restart and when an already-present admission is replayed.
- Provider refusal and task interruption cannot permanently suppress initiation.
- Retry is bounded and uses a stable idempotency key shared with the conversation ledger.
- Health or evidence exposes pending, retrying, completed, and terminal-failure dispositions.
- The autonomous initiation demo completes without an operator prompt and without duplicate greetings after uncertain completion.
