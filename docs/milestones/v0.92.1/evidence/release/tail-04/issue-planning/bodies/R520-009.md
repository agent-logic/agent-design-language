## Summary

Remediate P2 finding `T520-DEP-001` from internal review #520 by bounding the AWS and GCP XCL Terraform provider constraints to explicitly supported major versions. At reviewed candidate `c24f8fa65ce445b03ce6cd69007307291d78b60c`, both roots use open-ended `>= 5.0` constraints even though their lockfiles already select later majors.

## Goal

Make dependency resolution for both XCL Terraform roots predictable and reviewable across lock regeneration and fresh initialization.

## Required Outcome

The AWS and GCP XCL roots declare documented compatible provider-major ranges, their committed locks satisfy those ranges, and required validation rejects a future open-ended provider-major floor.

## Deliverables

- Compatible-major constraints for `hashicorp/aws` in `infra/aws/runtime/xcl-01/versions.tf` and `hashicorp/google` in `infra/gcp/workloads/xcl-01/versions.tf`.
- Lockfile updates only where the selected versions must change to satisfy the chosen supported ranges.
- A focused static regression check that rejects open-ended provider-major floors in these release roots.
- Validation evidence for read-only initialization, formatting, validation, and bounded plans for both roots.
- Updated dependency-support documentation if the selected major policy is not self-evident from the constraints.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Both XCL roots declare explicit supported provider-major ranges instead of only `>= 5.0`.
- The selected AWS and Google versions in the committed lockfiles satisfy the corresponding source constraints.
- A future breaking provider major is not eligible through routine lock regeneration without an intentional reviewed source change.
- The focused regression check fails on an open-ended major floor and passes on the checked-in constraints.
- No unrelated Terraform root or provider major is changed.
