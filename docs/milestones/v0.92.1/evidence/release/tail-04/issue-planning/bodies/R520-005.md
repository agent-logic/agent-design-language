## Summary

Repair the P1 repository-review packet defect that globally truncates evidence before lane routing and can assign no product code or tests to mandatory specialists.

## Goal

Make repository-review completeness derive from the full changed-path denominator and honest per-lane selection evidence.

## Required Outcome

At exact review candidate `c24f8fa65ce445b03ce6cd69007307291d78b60c`, findings `A520-ARCH-004` and `C520-CODE-004` show that the builder truncates globally before classifying lanes. The #520 scaffold consequently assigned identical lock files to unrelated reviewers and zero files to tests despite material implementation and test changes. The builder must classify the complete inventory first, retain per-lane denominator truth, and fail closed on materially empty or category-invalid lanes.

- Per-lane source denominator, selected denominator, exclusions, and digest.

- The #520 regression fixture yields nonempty, category-valid code, tests, security, architecture, documentation, and dependency assignments.
- Empty, category-invalid, and digest-mismatch fixtures fail deterministically.
