# Remaining v0.91.7 Sprint Reviews

Issue: #5403

## Objective

Produce retained, findings-first reviews for the closed v0.91.7 sprint
umbrellas whose current evidence is missing or materially incomplete.

## Review Model

Each sprint receives a separate packet covering its umbrella contract, ordered
children, merged changes, lifecycle truth, validation, retained evidence, and
residual risk. Applicable specialist lanes cover code, architecture, security,
tests, documentation, dependencies, and closeout evidence. A final synthesis
routes actionable findings to separate remediation issues and prepares the
canonical sprint-review register update after its current claim is released.

## Boundaries

- This issue reviews #4639, #4640, #4648, #5036, #5045, #5121, #5174,
  #5227, #5247, and #5276.
- It does not implement product, runtime, or lifecycle-tooling fixes.
- It does not review open v0.91.7 work or the active v0.91.8 implementation
  wave.
- Existing retained packets remain evidence inputs; they are not silently
  rewritten to manufacture missing historical proof.

## Completion

Completion requires ten separate review packets, a findings and remediation
register, a prepared canonical register reconciliation, and independent review
of the resulting review package.
