# Meet the AI Coworkers

The first episode of The Cognitive Stack introduces ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, then explores three everyday questions:

- What should an AI assistant do independently, and when should it ask?
- Who else is affected when software acts on our behalf?
- What happens when two assistants negotiate a meeting?

The conversation was authored sequentially by the three named models under an editorial brief. Claude revised two passages after accuracy review. Human script approval preceded synthesis. All voices are synthetic: OpenAI Coral reads ChatGPT, OpenAI Echo reads Gemini, and Deepgram Pluto reads Claude. Voice rendering does not establish model identity or consciousness.
