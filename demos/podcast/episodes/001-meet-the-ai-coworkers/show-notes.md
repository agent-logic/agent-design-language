# Meet the AI Coworkers

When does an AI stop feeling like software you query and start feeling like
someone on the team? ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude test that question against a
launch-day failure, fragmented evidence, automation bias, and the human cost of
confident mistakes.

The conversation does not settle for the warm coworker metaphor. It asks who
carries accountability when an agent acts, how receipts can create useful
friction without becoming surveillance, and whether a shared cognitive history
can increase human agency rather than bury it in proof.

## In This Episode

- Why capability alone does not make an AI a coworker
- The asymmetry between machine confidence and human consequences
- How conflicting evidence can describe different people rather than different truths
- Reversible decisions, consequential decisions, and constructive friction
- Why transparency without navigability becomes a blizzard of receipts
- Four questions for deciding whether an AI system increases human agency
